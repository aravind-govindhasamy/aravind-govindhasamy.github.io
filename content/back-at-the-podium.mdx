## What I Talked About

The title was **Circuit Digest & The Age of AI-Assisted Development for Innovations**.

The slides are public: [ppt.budeglobal.in/p/ai-powered-development-2026-humanized](https://ppt.budeglobal.in/p/ai-powered-development-2026-humanized/)

I did not want to give the usual talk. Twenty-five AI tools on a slide, developers are finished, thank you and good afternoon. I wanted to talk about what I actually deal with at work.

The shift is not that AI can write code. We have known that for a while.

The shift is that AI has started to take part in the work. Reading a repository. Changing files. Running commands. Running the tests. Reading the errors and trying again.

Autocomplete, then chat, then assistants inside the IDE, and now agents. The word that changed is agency. It is not only answering anymore. It acts.

## Real Systems, Not Demos

Then I moved to the part I know best. At Semicon Media, the systems I work on look like this:

**Hardware → MQTT → backend → database → dashboard → alerts.**

Real devices sending real data. And when something breaks, you do not get a polite message saying the system has stopped working. You get "why is this device offline?" at the worst possible hour.

![Presenting the Circuit Digest Cloud MCP slide](/blog/back-at-the-podium/presenting-mcp-slide.jpg)

This is where I showed the MCP server for Circuit Digest Cloud. Instead of opening a dashboard and hunting through device lists, you can ask in plain English:

> Which devices are offline?

The interesting part is not that the model understands the question. It is that the model does not get the keys to everything. You expose a controlled set of tools, and it works out which ones it needs. That boundary is the whole design.

## Four Documents Before You Let an Agent Build

The practical section, and the one I care about most.

1. **PRD** — what are we building?
2. **User flow** — how does someone actually use it?
3. **Technical specification** — how are we building it?
4. **AI coding rules** — what should the agent not do?

"Build me an IoT tracking application" is not a specification. That is telling someone to go somewhere and come back with something.

The line I kept returning to:

> AI moves the typing. It does not move the responsibility.

Someone still has to decide whether the output is correct, and that someone is not the model. A wrong answer arrives with perfect grammar and full confidence.

I also admitted that the deck itself was built with AI assistance. If a slide looked well made, that helped. If a slide looked strange, that was still me.

## The One O'Clock Slot

Now the honest part.

The session started at 1 PM. Right after lunch.

I have sat in that chair. At 1 PM the eyes are open and the operating system has already shut down. Somewhere in that hall, someone was weighing agentic engineering against sleep, and sleep was ahead on points.

So I kept it moving. Examples, real projects, screenshots, the demo, a joke when the room went too quiet. Anything to hold the room until 3.

## Two Years Since the Last One

This was my fourth session, and the first in about two years. In order:

1. **My PG days, inside college.** A session on GitHub for students — how to use it, what it is good for day to day, and how to put a portfolio together.
2. **UIT Coimbatore.** My first session outside my own college, through a referral from a friend.
3. **Way2me.** Online classes.
4. **PSG CAS, 18 September 2026.** This one.

The gap between the third and the fourth is about two years. The distance between the first and the fourth is longer than the dates suggest. In that GitHub session, I was teaching people how to save their work properly. This time I was talking about tools that write part of the work for you.

Two years is not a long time. It was enough.
