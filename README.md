### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Full-Stack Developer & Platform Engineer** with 4+ years of hands-on experience designing, developing, and operating mission-critical production systems that bridge the physical and digital worlds — from low-level serial byte-streams and RFID reader protocols to high-throughput cloud backends and responsive web/mobile apps.

- 🚀 **Currently Engineering**: **[Circuit Digest Cloud](https://www.circuitdigest.cloud/)** at Semicon Media — architecting end-to-end cloud IoT microservices (FastAPI, asynchronous MQTT, GeoLinker GPS fleet tracking with geofencing, computer vision APIs, Razorpay payments, and Airtel M2M SIM lifecycle automation).
- 🏛️ **Initiative**: Founder & Lead Architect at **[BUDE Global](https://www.budeglobal.in/)** — operating 11 self-hosted production applications on on-premise Dell PowerEdge T40 hardware fronted by Cloudflare Zero-Trust edge ingress (`cloudflared`) and Nginx reverse proxies.
- 🎓 **Doctoral Research**: Ph.D. Research Scholar in Computer Science at **PSG College of Arts and Science (PSGCAS)**, Coimbatore.
- 🏢 **Previous Experience**:
  - **PSGR Krishnammal College for Women**: ERP Assistant Programmer — RFID Gate Software Ecosystem (5,000+ daily patrons), GLPI IT Asset Management (1,800+ nodes), and Open Journal Systems (OJS) editorial publishing platform.
  - **2CQR Automation Private Limited**: Software Developer (2.5 years) — engineered RFID library automation, iSmartShelf self-checkout kiosks, SIP2 protocol bridges, and ILMS database integrations (KOHA, AutoLib, SOUL, eGrantalaya) across 20+ premier universities (SRM, VIT Chennai, Crescent, PSG Tech).
